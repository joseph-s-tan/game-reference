Prophesy of Pendor 3.9.5 · Sher-first route

Develop the company before building the kingdom.

Sher’s companion-first approach is the route spine: concentrate early experience, add soldiers when they expand the fights you can win, then turn mercenary freedom into a defensible first fief. The precise relation thresholds and 3.9.5 additions below fill gaps in that sequence.

The route in six moves

1 · Seed capital

Keep the party small

Let the player and a few useful companions take most early kills, experience, and loot. Add companions when you can equip them well enough to expand the set of profitable fights.

2 · Develop companions

Practice before field armies

Fight bandits and practice battles, quest along the way, and build a stable roster. Sher’s practical transition marker is a combat core near level 20 with 200+ one-handed proficiency; it is a readiness marker for the core, not a requirement for every utility companion.

3 · Bridge force

Add only enough troops to open new targets

Five to ten Adventurers can grow into a small Hero Adventurer reserve without turning the companion-development party into a wage-heavy army. Expand further when lord battles become productive.

4 · Mercenary company

Use wars for money and relations

A contract subsidizes the army while repeated allied victories build lord relations. Switch employers when war geography makes a better first fief possible.

5 · Vassalage

Take a defensible first castle

Enter when war, troop capacity, a reserved Qualis Gem, and political support line up. Roughly 200 party capacity can work under favorable conditions; 300–350 is a more comfortable castle-taking range. Start the CKO training clock once the fief is secured.

6 · Independence

Rebel with reserves and allies

Hold a compact perimeter, store recruits, develop trainers and CKO troops, and build relations with desirable future lords. Rebel when the kingdom can absorb its first concentrated war, rather than when a calendar date arrives.

Those numbers describe Sher’s optimization route. They are not hard game gates. The selected QoL Plus v2 module changes companion Prisoner Management capacity, so check the roster total before committing player skill points.

Captured-lord defection uses a different check

Once you are king, the selected QoL Plus v2 prisoner dialogue requires relation strictly greater than:

15 + campaign AI modifier + notable bonus + enemy castles + 2 × enemy towns − min(your kingdom’s castles, 5) − 2 × min(your kingdom’s towns, 3) − Persuasion

Campaign AI adds 10 Good / 5 Average / 0 Poor. The first three numbered lords of each major kingdom add 10; other lords add zero. Villages do not count. Your kingdom’s territory discount caps at 11. A calculated threshold of 31 requires relation 32. This is the installed module’s prisoner check, separate from persuading a free lord.

What fills gaps in Sher’s guide

The Old Tale stronghold

The 3.9.5 questline offers a garrison before your first castle. It changes the cost of storing irreplaceable troops during the companion phase; use it when available without treating early vassalage as the only storage solution.

Noldor end state

Elacrai opens at +30 Noldor relation. The in-game victory screen requires Favorable or better Noldor relations, so any hostile farming phase needs a planned exit before the final push.
